# PRODUCT.md — Red Sizzle

## Product
Red Sizzle is a premium fitness media company producing professional weightlifting
and strength-training educational videos. The product's promise: precision-driven
training knowledge — technique first, measurable progress.

## Audience
Lifters of every level — beginners learning fundamentals, intermediates refining
technique — who want expert, detail-obsessed instruction rather than hype.

## Job to be done
- Visitor understands within seconds what Red Sizzle is and why it is different
  (precision, knowledge, progress).
- Visitor can browse the offered value (training approach, features, programs)
  and act: explore training or get in touch.

## Success
A first-time visitor leaves knowing: what Red Sizzle teaches, that it is
technique-first, and how to start (contact / explore training).

## Brand (pinned by brief)
- Colors: Primary Red `#F0203B`, Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Deep Navy `#292C40`
  (dominant background), Secondary Dark `#202233`, Muted Gray `#A5A8B5`.
- Typography: bold condensed display for headlines (Oswald), clean sans body (Inter).
- Logo: provided Red Sizzle lockup — bold condensed red "RED" with an extended
  leg/underline bar, white condensed slightly-slanted "SIZZLE". Horizontal for nav,
  stacked for vertical compositions. Text placeholder permitted if asset unavailable.
- Voice: precise, confident, coach-like. No hype, no gamification.

## Constraints
- Single page, exactly 7 sections (Hero, About, Features, Training, Results, FAQ,
  Contact) + nav + footer. English only.
- Statistics are fictional brand content (250+ videos, 15K+ learners, 40+ programs,
  95% satisfaction).
- Contact form is demo-only: must never falsely claim a message was delivered.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. WCAG 2.2 AA.
